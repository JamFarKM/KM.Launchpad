05

Keyboard focus

Nothing is focus-styled today. With this many small controls it's both an accessibility requirement and a polish win. Flip the toggle in the top bar, then Tab through these.

2px --accent, 2px offset, and crucially :focus-visible rather than :focus — so it appears for keyboard users and never on a mouse click.
